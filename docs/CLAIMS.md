# Claim Filing Process

## Overview
This guide provides merchants with a step-by-step process to file claims for insured shipments.

## Prerequisites
- Shipment Number
- Proof of Value
- Supporting Documentation

## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Getting Started
1. Navigate to the **Support** section in the merchant dashboard.
2. Click on **File a Claim** under the **Claims** subsection.

### 2. Filing a Claim
1. **Provide Shipment Details**
   - Enter the shipment number associated with the insured package.
   - Select the type of claim (e.g., loss, damage, delay).

2. **Upload Required Documents**
   - **Proof of Value**: Upload a document proving the value of the lost or damaged item (e.g., receipt, invoice, appraisal).
   - **Supporting Documentation**: Include any additional evidence (e.g., photos of damage, delivery confirmation).

3. **Describe the Incident**
   - Provide a detailed description of what happened.
   - Include the date and location of the incident (if applicable).

4. **Review and Submit**
   - Double-check all information for accuracy.
   - Click **Submit Claim** to send your request.

### 3. Tracking Your Claim
1. After submission, you'll receive a confirmation email with a reference number.
2. Use the **Notifications** section to track the status of your claim.
3. Our team will review your claim and contact you with further updates.

### 4. Claim Status Updates
- **Pending Review**: We are evaluating your claim and gathering necessary information.
- **Under Investigation**: We are investigating the incident to determine the appropriate course of action.
- **Approved**: The claim has been approved, and you will receive compensation.
- **Rejected**: The claim was not approved due to insufficient evidence or policy violations.

### 5. Receiving Compensation
- Once your claim is approved, you'll be notified of the compensation amount and payment method.
